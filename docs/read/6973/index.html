# Characteristics of the Head

Any woman whose... head is thui (sloping/tapered at the back)

or, as it is commonly called, a protruding forehead. If the protrusion in front and the protrusion behind answer to each other on both sides, that is all the better. It is a sign showing that she is a person of mindfulness and intelligence, clever and quick-witted. Even if it does not protrude on both sides — if it protrudes only at the back alone, that is still good enough.

Any woman whose... head is shaped like an elephant's head

or is ridged in undulations like a palmyra fruit It is a sign showing that she will have prosperity and flourishing in the future; she is a person of wasana (merit-destiny), and will become great and eminent in days to come.

Any woman whose... head is pom (short and rounded/stout)

It is a sign of a person given to rambling reverie, who likes to dream along with her moods, who cannot be taken seriously in anything; she is a chatterer, a braggart, and given to showing off. But if she makes her living in the way of trade, she has enough of a path to prosper by.

English

Any woman whose... head is paen (flattened)

It is a sign of a person who has wiles and tricks for taking advantage of her friends. She will be able to keep herself alive, but not in prosperity; at best only at a middling level, or sometimes up and down, not to be counted upon for certain.

Any woman whose... head is long

It is a sign of a person whose wits are not quick enough for others, slow — but who has honesty and loyal devotion toward those who have been good to her. If the head is long but large, she will prosper and flourish in the future. If it is long and small as well, there will be only days of hardship; she will not make a living as well as other people do.

Any woman whose... head is short and stubby

It is a sign of a person of quick wits, clever, sharp, who sees through her friends — but who will find it very hard to help herself rise to prosperity. She is one who loves fun, jesting, boisterous, cheerful, and broad-hearted; she is liked and cared for by friends and companions, and is desired by the men who come to associate with her.

Any woman whose... head is triangular in shape

It is a sign of a person who is diligent and vigorous, persevering and enduring at every kind of work. Whether life turns out good or bad lies with herself, in the manner of the saying that one is one's own refuge. To hope to borrow another person's nose to breathe with will come to nothing.

English

Any woman whose... head is square

It is a sign of a clever person, whose wits are as quick as other people's; she is her own counsellor in the conduct of her life — that is, if she chooses the right road, the future will meet with nothing but happiness and prosperity, but if she chooses the wrong road she will fall into hardship and misery. This refers to every kind of work, and also to the choosing of a spouse.

Any woman whose... head is round

It is a sign of a person who is honest and upright; her circumstances in life will be of a middling degree.

Any woman whose... crown of the head is high

or, as it is commonly called, a high "palace-peak" (chom prasat sung) It is a sign of a person full of ideas, clever and steadfast; she is a person of merit and wasana (destiny), and will have prosperity in the future.

Any woman whose... head juts out at the back of the skull like a hook

It is a sign of a restless person, who likes to romp about after her own nature. In the matter of occupation, she is usually fond of the various crafts, such as sewing, embroidery and needlework, or other handiwork.

Any woman whose... head has a bulging lump of flesh at the back

It is a sign of a person of boisterous nature, fond of children, and fond of play; she likes to do outlandish things, always eccentric and unlike others; she is a person who does not fret about the future, living along from one day to the next, in the way people call "whatever will do".

Any woman whose... sides of the head have bulging flesh

It is a sign of a person who tends to be in trouble in matters of money constantly. Even if she has a place to dwell, it will not be hers by right, or she will not have full right in that place.

Any woman whose... head is large

It is a sign of a person straitened in her thinking, in trouble over making a living. For the most part her life is beset with hardship, straitened in the matter of money and of dwelling; she looks upon the future as darkness, until at times she loses heart, without any spirit left to undertake any enterprise at all. Only if someone gives her encouragement will she muster perseverance again. For the greater part of her life she must depend upon a husband.

English

Characteristics of the Hair

Any woman... whose hair is black and fine

with smooth, clear skin, wide eyes, sweet speech, and a figure befitting a woman — this is a sign that she is a person who prospers in worldly wealth; whatever man takes her as his wife, his future will flourish and prosper.

Any woman... whose hair is burnt-brown in colour, and is oiled and lustrous

— this is a sign that she is long-lived, and strict in matters of custom and tradition; easy to instruct, easy to teach.

Any woman... whose hair is burnt-brown in colour

but is not oiled and not lustrous — this is a sign of a person who is long-lived, of full and strong body, bold of speech in matters that have reason, ardent in love, and excessive in sexual passion; not inclined toward study and the seeking of knowledge, ruthless of heart, lacking in loving-kindness and compassion.

เป็นเครื่องหมายของคนที่ไม่มีศีลธรรม ขาดความกรุณาปราณี คบกับ ใครก็คิดแต่จะเอาเปรียบ แม้ผมลักษณะเดียวกันนี้ ถ้าหยิกหยอง (หยิกธรรมชาติ) ยิ่งร้ายขึ้นไปกว่านั้น เป็นคนที่คบค้าสมาคมด้วยไม่ได้ อย่างเด็ดขาด

หญิงใด...เส้นผมดำเป็นมัน

และมีเส้นตรงเป็นกระเชิง อ่อนละเอียด เป็นเครื่องหมายของคนมีใจกรุณา ยุติธรรม สัตย์ซื่อ ไม่ประพฤติร้าย ต่อใครก่อน จะมั่นคงต่อสามีของตนเสมอต้นเสมอปลาย

หญิงใด...เส้นผมเป็นสีน้ำตาล ทั้งหยาบทั้งแข็ง

เป็นเครื่องหมายของคนที่เลี้ยงยาก มีความซื่อตรงน้อยมักคะนองใน ทางที่ผิด แม้ผมลักษณะเดียวกันนี้ ถ้าหยิก (หยิกแต่กำเนิด) จะเป็น คนที่อ่อนแอ ปราศจากไหวพริบ

English

Any woman... who has a pointed tuft of hair at the front

or what is commonly called a "turtle's tail" — this is a sign of a person whose father will die before her mother, or else that the two will be parted from each other.

Any woman... whose hair is fine and thin

— this is a sign of a person without moral virtue, lacking in mercy and kindness; whomever she associates with, she thinks only of taking advantage of them. Even with hair of this same character, if it is frizzy and fuzzy (frizzy by nature) it is worse still than that: such a person absolutely cannot be associated with.

Any woman... whose hair is black and oily, and frizzy and unruly

(frizzy and unruly by nature) — this is a sign of a person hot-tempered, cunning in her cleverness, lacking in fairness, and violent in sexual passion; a self-seeking person who takes advantage of people in general.

Any woman... whose hair is black and oily

and has straight strands standing bristly, soft and fine — this is a sign of a person with a compassionate heart, fair, honest and loyal, who does not do harm to anyone first; she will be steadfast to her husband, constant from beginning to end.

Any woman... whose hair is brown in colour, both coarse and stiff

— this is a sign of a person who is hard to raise, having little uprightness, given to acting rashly in wrongful ways. Even with hair of this same character, if it is frizzy (frizzy from birth) she will be a person who is weak, devoid of quick wit.

Any woman... whose hair is coarse, straight and stiff

— this is a sign of a person excessive in sexual passion, lacking in mindfulness and in any thought of caring for her own good name. However much property she has, she spends it without restraint, without a thought for the days ahead; her future will meet with nothing but hardship and trouble.

Any woman... whose hair is fine and soft, brown in colour, both abundant and thick

Any woman... whose hairline stands very far from the ears

— this is a sign of a person carrying the fruit of past deeds committed in a former age; her life will meet with misfortune and with hardship and bitter difficulty. However much she toils and wearies herself, she cannot prosper; there will be nothing but being oppressed and slandered by others, and she will be short-lived.

Any woman... whose hairline lies very close to the ears

— this is a sign that she is a person of quick wit, clever, of good intelligence, and diligent and vigorous, good at taking up work; a person of merit and fortune whose future will flourish and prosper, and who will be long-lived — at the least not below 80 years and upward.

English

Characteristics of the Shape of the Face

Any woman... whose face is shaped like the face of the sky

— this is a sign that she is a person of gentle disposition, loving peace, fond of dressing showily, and fond of associating with people without choosing by rank; yet she has a compassionate spirit and does not like to take unfair advantage, does not envy those better off than herself; she is generally beloved by those who meet her. Even if she falls into hardship there will be people to support her. In matters of sexual passion things proceed in a plain and even way, without extravagance.

Any woman... whose face is shaped like the face of a monkey

— this is a sign that she is a person of coarse disposition, fond of doing odd and freakish things contrary to ordinary human beings, fierce of heart, without mercy, her manners not within the standards of well-bred people. A woman of this character, if she takes a husband, tends to bring shame and loss of face upon her husband repeatedly, because of her disposition to conduct herself unlike everyone else, and she has a promiscuous sexual passion, in which no constancy can be found.

English

Any woman... whose face resembles a pig's face

It is a sign showing that she is a person of wavering disposition; whatever she undertakes she does not think through carefully, and only once the mistake has been made does she come to regret it. Her character appears to be that of a person of guile and cunning stratagems with her friends and companions; she is fond of showing off and of talking herself up while putting others down. Whatever she undertakes seldom succeeds.

Any woman... whose face resembles a bear's face

It is a sign showing that she is clever and well-informed, but at times cruel-hearted; once angered at someone she binds herself in vengefulness. She respects her own opinions and does not readily listen to anyone's advice or admonition. Whatever she undertakes she usually aims to carry through to success; if it does not succeed she does not abandon her intention.

Any woman... whose face resembles a gibbon's face

It is a sign showing that she is a person who loves justice and loves peace. Her nature is quiet and demure, to the point that she looks like a person given to sorrow. She likes to think and to dream far off. She has honesty and dislikes taking advantage of others. Her sustaining of life is uncertain — one moment having, one moment poor. But if any man gets her as a wife, she will be steadfast, loyal and devoted to her husband forever.

Any woman... whose face resembles a goat's face

It is a sign showing that she is clever, of high intelligence and mindfulness, and loves peace. Her future holds nothing but a path of prosperity, and she will attain the good by her own self. In the early stages she will usually receive help from elders, but in the end she relies on herself and advances by her own self.

Any woman... with a downturned face

It is a sign showing that she is a person who does not go in for friends and company; she likes to live alone in contented solitude. She does things simply, without ceremony or fuss; she is not high-aspiring nor extravagant, and is not a spendthrift. When she is to decide upon undertaking anything, even in matters of love, she usually consults the elders whom she respects. In the early period her life is not very smooth; only in the later period does it gradually improve.

Any woman... with a large round facial contour

It is a sign showing that she is a person who holds her own reasoning supreme, likes to behave in a childish way, does things without deliberating carefully, following only her own whim. She is frivolous, does nothing in earnest, and likes to put herself forward, volunteering to do work for other people.

Any woman... with a long face

It is a sign showing that she is a person who does not like to take advantage of others, and does not like people who are grasping over small and petty things. She is generous and open-handed toward those in hardship; she is a person of moral virtue. She will be merry at times, but she has composure and gentleness constantly. If she has a husband, she will be beloved of her husband and esteemed by people generally.

English

Any woman... with a flat face

It is a sign showing that she is a person of quick-witted, clever thought, cool-headed and steady, but who usually does not deliberate carefully before acting. Only once she has done wrong does she realise it afterwards — falling into the saying that runs, "the ox is lost, and only then is the pen fenced in." But she is a person with charm about her, rather flirtatious, and easy in making acquaintances. In the latter part of life she must feed herself by her own shanks.

Any woman... with a short face

It is a sign showing that she is a person with an envious heart, bold of mouth, who likes to do things in a boastful way with ทิษฐิ [thitthi — conceited stubbornness of opinion; so spelled on the page, for ทิฐิ]. She holds herself supreme, takes advantage of other people, but will not let go of what is her own. She is a person whose friendships do not last: at first all is well, but once others come to know her character, they break off with her.

Any woman... whose face is always smiling

or, as it is called, having constant cheerfulness It is a sign showing that she is a person of bright spirits; her future will be complete and fruitful in wealth, and she will be beloved of her husband. But if she has a dimple in the cheek, she is usually one who causes her husband vexation of mind on account of her own flirtatiousness, her disposition being unsteady, and she gets acquainted with people easily. Her way of life in the future, at the best, is only enough to get by; riches she will not find.
