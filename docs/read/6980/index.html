Chapter: Homage to the Teacher

[Pali chant, given in Thai transliteration] He who sat at the foot of the noble Bodhi tree, having conquered Mara together with his army, attained the great Awakening — boundless knowledge, transcending all worlds. By the power of this truth, may there be glorious victory; the Wheel of the Dhamma turns. We invite Lord Ishvara to come and stand at [our] left side; we invite Lord Narayana to come and stand at [our] right side. We invite the [River] Ganges to become [our] saliva; we invite the Wind God to become the breath of [our] mouth. We invite the Naga king to become [our] necklace and ornaments. Whatever I speak, may it be accomplished — may [my] undertakings succeed, may [my] deeds succeed, may [my] works succeed. Thus has the Tathagata come; may glory and fire-power succeed, may victory be constant, may gain succeed without end; may every deed be accomplished.

(The auspicious and forbidden times for candle-lighting and candle-veneration)

Table, two sides — "Venerating on the waxing moon" and "Venerating on the waning moon" — listing, for each lunar night from 1 to 15, whether it is auspicious (คี) or inauspicious (บ่คี) and the resulting fortune, e.g. night 1 on both sides: inauspicious, will bring suffering; night 2 waxing: auspicious, will bring the kindness and affection of others, while night 2 waning: inauspicious, will bring later trouble; and so on through night 15, ending on the waning side with "auspicious — good fortune will pour in."

(The monthly unlucky days on which no auspicious rites should be performed)

Table listing lunar months grouped in three (e.g. months 1/6/10) against ruling days: months 1, 6, 10 are unlucky on Tuesday (Sun and Moon [group heading, Angkarn/Mars]); months 3, 7, 11 are unlucky on Saturday together with Thursday; months 4, 8, 12 are unlucky on Friday together with Wednesday.

Textbook: Formula for the Candle-Veneration Incantation

A calculation table with columns Day, Split, Suppress, Chief, Enter, Die, giving a numeric offset for each of the seven days of the week — used to work out, from the day in question, which of these five conditions applies.

This, then, is to be remembered as the formula for candle-veneration to be used beforehand.

That is to say, whatever person intends to go, intends to act, in advance of the rite, must have that rite [performed] — on that day, following the day's calculation for that day, so that it is correct according to the method handed down by the teachers and masters of old; only then, having reckoned that rite accordingly, will it be brought to successful completion. นะโมพุทธายะ นะขอจงมาบังเกิดเป็นคุณพระเจ้า โมขอจงมาบังเกิดเป็นคุณ พระสัทธรรมะเจ้า พุทธขอจงมาบังเกิดเป็นคุณพระสังฆะเจ้า ธาจงบังเกิดเป็นคุณบิดา มารดา ยะขอจงมาบังเกิดเป็นคุณครูบาอาจารย์ทุกพระองค์ ของจงมาประสิทธิให้แก่ ข้าพเจ้าสมความปรารถนา แห่งข้าพเจ้าทุกประการ ณ บัดนี้ข้าพเจ้า (ชื่อเรา) ได้ นำเอาพระมหายันต์เทียนต้นนี้มากระทำการสักการะบูชา ต่อหน้าพระพุทธเจ้า พระ ธรรมเจ้า พระสังฆเจ้า คุณบิดามารดา และคุณครูบาอาจารย์เจ้า เพื่อจักขอให้กลิ่น ธูปควันเทียนสัพพะเวทย์มหายันต์เทียนเหล่านี้ ไปถึงหัวใจกายตัวผู้ที่มีชื่อว่า (ชื่อของ คู่ครอง) ให้เขามารัก มาหลง มาใหล ให้จิตใจของเขาฝ่าหน้าแต่ข้าเจ้า ทุกวันทุกเมื่อ ทุกเช้าทุกค่ำ แม้นจะนั่งก็บ่อมิได้ นอนก็บ่อมิได้ กินก็บ่อมิได้ ยืนก็บ่อมิได้ ให้พร่ำ เพ้อละเมอ หลงใหลหาแต่ข้าเจ้า (ชื่อของเรา) ดังบทบาทพระคาถาแห่งครูบา อาจารย์ได้กล่าวไว้ว่า

นามมานังสะมาโส ยุดตะโถ ยุดถัตถะ แห่งนามะ (ชื่อของคู่ครอง) พุทธังเอหิ มา ริโส จิตตังมา ธัมมัง เอหิ มาริโสตังมา สังฆัง มาริโสจิตตังมา บุพผังมา กันธังมา ราชะกุมาโรมา ราชะกุมารีมา อิตถีมา ปุริโสมา ปุริจามา ปะริเทวันตี ปิยังมามะ มะ อมจิตตัง จิตจะนะ นังมามะมัด อุงพุทธะเสโท อิติปิโส ภะคะวา อะอุอัง ธัมมะ เสโถ ภะคะวา อะอุอัง สังฆะเสโฐ อิติปิโส ภะคะวาวัณณะสักโก ปิยะอมโห มุกขัง มุกขัง สะตะมาลัง (ชื่อของคนรัก) อมมะณีรักสวาหุมติด ติดด้วย นะโมพุทธายะ

7

Incantation for Lighting a Candle Before the Buddha Image

Namo, three times.

Namo Buddhaya. [Interpreting the syllable Na:] May there come to be born in me the virtue of the Buddha. [Mo:] May there come to be born in me the virtue of the True Dhamma. [Phut:] May there come to be born in me the virtue of the Sangha. [Tha:] May there be born in me the virtue of father and mother. [Ya:] May there come to be born in me the virtue of every teacher and master — may they grant success to me according to my every wish, in every respect. Now I, ( my name ), have taken this great candle-yantra and performed an act of veneration before the Buddha, the Dhamma, the Sangha, [the virtue of] father and mother, and [the virtue of] my teachers and masters, so as to ask that the scent of incense and candle-smoke of this all-powerful great candle-yantra reach the heart and body of the one named ( name of the partner ), causing him/her to love me, to become infatuated, to be swept away, so that his/her heart turns toward none but me, every day, at all times, every morning and every evening — that he/she cannot even sit still, cannot sleep, cannot eat, cannot stand, but babbles and dreams, infatuated, seeking only me, ( my name ) — according to the verse of the incantation spoken by the teachers and masters, as follows:

[Pali-style formula, given in Thai transliteration, invoking the name of the beloved together with the Buddha, Dhamma, Sangha, and various figures — princes, princesses, men, women — to come and be drawn, infatuated and yearning, toward the speaker, sealed with] "...may [name of the loved one] be bound with the gem of love, bound fast, sealed with Namo Buddhaya."

If there is any further wish, it may be prayed for at this point. Light 5 sticks of incense.

Incantation for Lighting a Candle to Sever [a Couple] Apart

[Incantation opening with the sacred syllable Om, addressing a spirit-mother/hag figure] Om, mother of the striped dog-spirit, mother of the water-ghost, mother of the moaning ghost — I shall undo and strip off your covering garment, cover your head so you sleep. I shall sever ( the man ... and the woman ... ) so that they flee from one another. I shall sever the water so that it flees down the stream; I shall sever the stream; I shall sever ( the man ... and the woman ... ) so that they flee from one another — sealed with Om, savaha, child.

Om, earth-spirit of the earth, pig[-like] earth-spirit, [invoking imagery of two forked/branching things] making the two [people] like a pig's [divided] tail and forehead, two forks ( the man ... and the woman ... ), parting from one another like water parting at a river-fork into two branches ( the man ... and the woman ... ) — then let them go blind to one another, ( the man ... and the woman ... ), let their arms break toward one another, ( the man ... and the woman ... ) — should they love one another, let lightning strike their heads instead; sealed with Om savaha, child.

Om, let the [forked] imagery hold: I shall take you both, lord ( the man ... and the woman ... ), and part you from one another; you ( the man ... and the woman ... ), part from one another; you ( the man ... and the woman ... ) — turn so that the mirror between you shatters, so that the yoke between you snaps apart. Om, sever, part, away, away.

Om, [imagery of] a vine severed, a gourd-stem severed — I shall sever apart ( the man ... and the woman ... ) from one another, like a dog scattering and severing the rope ( the man ... and the woman ... ), leaving a hundred knots between them, laid apart. Om savaha.

(Recite the whole three times, then pray according to what is wished.)

๑.เขียนชื่อ นามสกุล วัน เดือน พ.ศ. ปีเกิด อายุ ตามตัวอย่าง

๔.ปลุกฤทธิ์ยันต์ด้วย พุทโธเมนิสิทธิเม สิเสยัญญัง เมโทจะประมัดตะมังยัญญะ สัตถาเทวะมนุสสานะยัญญัง ภาทังจะอุตตะมัง ธัมมังจะสะระณังคะโตยัญญัง เมทะ นิมะหิทธิโก สังฆังจะสะระณังคะโตยัญญัง เมทะนิมะหิทธิโก อิติปาเลติ

๕.ฟั่นเป็นเทียนจุด ก่อนจะฟั่นเทียนให้ทำไส้เทียนก่อนด้วยการใช้สายสิญจน์เกินอายุผู้ ที่จะจุดเทียน 1 เส้น ม้วนไส้เทียนแล้ว เอาขี้ผึ้งแผ่นตากแดดให้อ่อนพอประมาณ กะ เอาเอง แล้วมาฟั่นเป็นเทียนให้มีหัวมีท้ายด้วยเหมือนเทียนทั่วไป แล้วเอาแช่ช่องฟิต ให้เทียนแข็งตัวเร็วๆ

Yantra Craft: The Naga-King Candle for Dispelling Misfortune

1. Write [the practitioner's] given name, surname, day, month, Buddhist Era year, year of birth, and age, following the example [given elsewhere].

2. Recite the yantra formula: "The yantra syllables are as brief as [the phrase] uppājati; the yantra syllables are as brief as vikiriṅkare. The yantra numerals are as brief as uppājati; the yantra numerals are as brief as vikiriṅkare."

3. Also recite the name-formula: "Nāmaṁ [taken] together is fitting, fittingly meant" — of all such names — by the power of the teaching Master. This candle-yantra should accordingly be called "the Naga-King Candle-Yantra for Dispelling Misfortune." [Formula:] "anuppatiṭṭhānaṁ vuttayogato" — whatever example fails to succeed, the teaching Master shall bring to completion by means of this formula.

4. Awaken the yantra's power with: "Buddho me nisiddhi me, sise yaññaṁ, medo ca pamattamaṅ-yaññaṁ, satthā devamanussānaṁ yaññaṁ, bhātaṅ ca uttamaṁ, dhammaṁ ca saraṇaṁ gato yaññaṁ, medanimahiddhiko, saṅghaṁ ca saraṇaṁ gato yaññaṁ, medanimahiddhiko" — thus it protects.

5. Roll it into a candle to be lit. Before rolling the candle, first make the wick using a length of sacred thread (sai sin) longer than the age of the person for whom the candle will be lit, one strand. Having rolled the wick, take a sheet of beeswax, sun it until moderately softened — judge this by feel — then roll it into a candle with a head and a tail, as with an ordinary candle. Then place it in a fitted groove/mould so that the candle sets firm quickly.

6. Consecrate it with this incantation:

"Whether from a king, or from a thief, or from a human, or from a non-human, or from fire, or from water, or from a demon, or from a stump, or from a thorn, or from the stars/season, or from an epidemic disease, or from false teaching, or from wrong view, or from a bad person; the moon['s] elephant, horses, deer, oxen... English

Technique of the yaksha-suppressing candle yantra

1. Write the name, surname, day, month, year (B.E.) of birth, and age, following the example.

2. Recite the yantra formula: 'Akkhara-yan dang san dang uppatchati, akkhara-yan dang san dang wikringkhare, lekha-yan dang san dang uppatchati, lekha-yan dang san dang wikringkhare.'

3. Also recite the name-formula 'Nama-nang samaso yudtatho yudtatha' of all names — by the power and majesty [of the lineage] may the teacher-master intend this candle yantra to be called the yaksha-suppressing candle yantra. 'Anuppathitthanang wudtayokato' — whatever example has failed to succeed, may the teacher-master cause it to succeed by this formula.

4. Awaken the yantra's power with: 'Phuttho meni sitthi me, sise yanyang, metho cha pramatt tamang yanya, sattha thewa manutsasana yanyang, phatang cha uttamang, thammang cha saranang khatho yanyang, metha nimahitthiko, sangmang cha saranang khatho yanyang, metha nimahitthiko itipaleti.'

5. Twist the wax into a candle for lighting. Before twisting the candle, first make the wick using a length of sacred thread (sai sin) longer than the age of the person for whom the candle is to be lit, one wick. Roll the wick, then take a sheet of beeswax dried in the sun until soft enough — judge this yourself — then twist it into a candle with a head and a tail like an ordinary candle, then dip it in a mould/cooling channel so the candle hardens quickly.

6. Consecrate with this incantation: 'Om ma ung ti loma ti phutthachittang [gap] chittang ehimang thammachittang [gap] chittang ehimang sangkhachittang [gap] chittang ehimang manimang piyamama' (seven times). [The dotted gaps are printed as-is in the original, apparently marking an omitted or repeated portion of the chant.]

8. [The practitioner] should chant in worship before the Buddha image, sitting in meditation reciting the incantation until the candle burns out.
