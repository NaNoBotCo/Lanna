Photograph it · ส่องภาพ which kind does this look like?

Take or choose a picture. It is compared against the catalogue's free-licensed reference pictures and answered with kinds and their records. It names the kind. It never says a piece is genuine — that is a sian's work with the piece in hand.

Install this site (Add to Home Screen) and it appears in your phone's Share sheet: share any photo of an amulet straight here. Identification runs where the site is served with its identifier (tools/serve.py locally, or the wichaa Worker); on a plain static copy this page explains and offers text search instead.
