A child figure that is not a depiction but a home: a bound child-spirit, raised as family — fed sweet red drink and rice, named, spoken to, asked for help. The corpus holds the historical making in full, framed as ethnography, at manuscript 6983.

หุ่นพยนต์ · Hun Payon (animated effigy)กุมารทองเป็นเด็ก เลี้ยงอย่างลูก · หุ่นพยนต์เป็นผู้ใหญ่ถืออาวุธ ปลุกให้ทำงาน
A kuman is a child figure raised as family; a hun phayon is an armed adult effigy animated to serve. Both are fed, but one is a named child and the other a compelled animation.

A khun phaen is a pressed Buddha tablet kept like any amulet even when its material carries prai-kuman powder; a kuman thong is a child figure you feed.
