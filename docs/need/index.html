What a charm is for is the one thing a sale title never says — and English flattens it further: Thai names three different ways of not being harmed (คงกระพัน — the blade does not cut · มหาอุด — the shot does not fire · แคล้วคลาด — you were not standing there), and "protection" erases all three into one word. This directory keeps the distinctions. One shared term list is counted across both corpora — the treatises and 14,653 live listings — so you can also see which needs the tradition still teaches that the market no longer sells.

What these counts say — and all they say

A listing or a page is counted here when the emic term occurs verbatim in its text. That is the whole claim: not that the object works, not that a seller's word is good, not that a page is about the need. The vocabulary itself is kept in the project vault (), where every term carries its own confidence and the unverified ones say so out loud.
