Used to build place names. Not listed as a sense by the RID, but 92 of this head's dictionary-attested compounds are place-name definitions (ชื่อตำบล / ชื่ออำเภอ / ชื่อเขต). Strongly northern: 83 of the 5333 wats in the northern registry carry it, and 76 entries of the segmentation dictionary.

grew out of sense 1 ([RID sense 1 — no English yet]) — by metonymy · นามนัย: A place is named for the feature it has — the feature stands in for the location. That is metonymy rather than metaphor: the marsh, the field, the creek is actually there, and the name points at it. probable · น่าจะใช่
