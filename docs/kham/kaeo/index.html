ที่มา · etymology probable · น่าจะใช่

Native Tai. Proto-Tai *kɛːwꟲ, glossed both 'glass' and 'gem' — cognate with Lao ແກ້ວ, Northern Thai แก้ว, and Shan. Glass beads arrived in mainland Southeast Asia as trade goods and were themselves treasure, so the substance-sense and the gem-sense are not obviously separable in the ancestor.

คำไทดั้งเดิม ไทดั้งเดิม *kɛːwꟲ มีความหมายทั้ง 'แก้ว(วัสดุ)' และ 'อัญมณี' ตรงกับลาว ແກ້ວ คำเมือง แก้ว และไทใหญ่

คำเมือง · Northern: แก้ว ᨠᩯ᩶ᩅ — The Northern reflex is the same word, and it is heavily productive in Northern toponymy and ritual vocabulary — see sense 4 (ลูกแก้ว) and sense 8. unverified · ยังไม่ตรวจ

สะกด · spelling: Differs from แก้ (kae, 'to fix, to solve, to undo') only by the final ว — the commonest miss a learner makes with this word.

2a glass; tumbler; cup

ภาชนะสำหรับใส่น้ำดื่ม
daymaterialfoodverified · ตรวจแล้ว

A drinking vessel — by default glass, but the word survives the material, so a paper แก้ว and a plastic แก้ว are both ordinary Thai.

grew out of sense 1 (glass) — by metonymy · นามนัย: The substance names the thing made of it — the same move as English 'a glass'. Thai then took it one step further than English does, into the classifier. standard · ทั่วไป

3gem; jewel; crystal; precious stone

หินหรือแร่ที่มีค่า, รัตนะ
elevatedreligiousreligionroyaltymaterialverified · ตรวจแล้ว

A gem or jewel — the treasure-sense. Co-primary with sense 1 rather than derived from it: the Proto-Tai etymon already covers both, and which face came first is unsettled.

This is the sense that carries the word into Buddhist and royal language, and it is why แก้ว is everywhere in temple and place names. In Thai Buddhist vocabulary แก้ว is the native word that renders Pali ratana 'jewel' — so the Triple Gem, พระรัตนตรัย, is also spoken of as แก้วสามประการ or แก้วทั้งสาม, the three jewels.
คำประกอบ · compoundmeaning, and why
แก้วมณี
kaeo mani
jewel; gem
lit. gem-gem
A tautological compound: native แก้ว plus Indic มณี (< Pali/Skt maṇi), both meaning 'gem'. Pairing a native word with its Indic synonym is a standard Thai way of raising register, and this is a clean specimen of it.
verified · ตรวจแล้ว
แก้วแหวนเงินทอง
kaeo waen ngoen thong
valuables; worldly treasure; jewellery and money
lit. gems, rings, silver and gold
A fixed four-part phrase for material wealth as a category. It is not a list to be broken up — no one says แก้วแหวน alone.
phraseverified · ตรวจแล้ว
ลูกแก้ว
luk kaeo
crystal ball; glass marble; orb
lit. gem-child, using ลูก as the classifier of round things
The divination and the child's-toy readings share a form. For the ordination reading, which is a different sense entirely, see sense 4.
verified · ตรวจแล้ว
ดวงแก้ว
duang kaeo
orb; crystal sphere; a shining gem
lit. orb-gem
ดวง is the classifier of luminous round things — sun, moon, heart, fate — so ดวงแก้ว is a gem framed as something that shines.
standard · ทั่วไป
พระแก้วมรกต
phra kaeo morakot
the Emerald Buddha
lit. the venerable emerald gem
The palladium of the Thai kingdom. The image is jade — nephrite or jasper — not emerald; มรกต names the colour, not the stone. It reached Bangkok by way of the Lanna north, which is why the name saturates northern toponymy.
nameverified · ตรวจแล้ว
ร้อยแก้ว
roi kaeo
prose
lit. strung gems
Paired against ร้อยกรอง 'verse', literally 'strung-and-sieved'. The traditional reading is that prose strings its gems freely while verse strings them through a sieve of metre. Treat the motivation as what the tradition says about itself, not as established etymology.
traditional · ตามประเพณี

calques ratana · รัตนะ — แก้ว is the native Thai rendering of Pali ratana 'jewel'. This is the edge that joins the two halves of the old ThaiRoots data: the native bucket and the indic bucket were describing the same concept and never touched. verified · ตรวจแล้ว

4darling; treasured one; the precious one

ผู้เป็นที่รักยิ่ง, สิ่งที่รักยิ่ง
poeticdaykinshipreligionverified · ตรวจแล้ว

A person or thing held as treasure. Productive as a term of endearment and as an honorific for a child.

grew out of sense 3 (gem; jewel; crystal; precious stone) — by metaphor · อุปมา: What is precious as a gem is precious as a person. The metaphor is live enough that แก้ว can be attached to almost any beloved noun and be understood. standard · ทั่วไป

8Kaeo (as a personal name and a place-name element)

ใช้เป็นชื่อบุคคลและส่วนประกอบของชื่อสถานที่
dayelevatednameplaceverified · ตรวจแล้ว

One of the most productive naming elements in Thai and especially in the Lanna north — as a given name, a nickname, a royal style, and a component of temple, hill and water names.

grew out of sense 3 (gem; jewel; crystal; precious stone) — by metaphor · อุปมา: Naming a child or a place a gem is a blessing, not a description. This sense is the reason แก้ว saturates the northern map. standard · ทั่วไป
Counted in this project's own data rather than asserted: 59 of the segmentation dictionary's place-name entries carry แก้ว, and 138 of the 5,333 wats in the northern registry are named with it — ดอนแก้ว, ดอยแก้ว, ห้วยแก้ว, เวียงแก้ว, พระธาตุดอยแก้ว. It also heads Lanna noble styles: พญาแก้ว, ม่อนพญาแก้ว.

คำที่สับสนกันได้ · easily confused

  • แกว — แกว, mid tone, no tone mark, is an old ethnonym for the Vietnamese and is now offensive; it is not to be used. It shares this word's phonetic key แกว exactly, so the site's own search collapses the two — which is precisely why the entry has to say so. tone-onlyverified · ตรวจแล้ว
  • แก้ — แก้ 'to fix, solve, undo' differs only by the missing final ว. The commonest learner slip on this word, and the two are unrelated. spellingverified · ตรวจแล้ว

ยังต้องตรวจ · open questions

  • ⚑ แก้วสามประการ / แก้วทั้งสาม — register and currency for the Triple Gem; zero title hits here, needs page-text search.
