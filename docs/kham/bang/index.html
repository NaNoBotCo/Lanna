Heavy in the dictionary (56 place names) but nearly absent from the northern wat registry (1 of 5333), so it builds names somewhere other than the Lanna north. grew out of sense 1 ([RID sense 1 — no English yet]) — by metonymy · นามนัย: A place is named for the feature it has — the feature stands in for the location. That is metonymy rather than metaphor: the marsh, the field, the creek is actually there, and the name points at it. probable · น่าจะใช่
